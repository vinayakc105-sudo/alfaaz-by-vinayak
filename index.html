Chaandni Raat,
Dil Ke Alfaaz

Kuch baatein lafzon mein nahi,
ehsaason mein likhi jaati hain.

Shayari • Poetry • Mohabbat

Meri Shayari

MOHABBAT

Kuch chehre bas dekhe jaate hain, Aur kuch dil mein utar jaate hain. Kuch mulaqatein pal bhar ki hoti hain, Magar unke kisse umr bhar reh jaate hain.

— Vinayak Chaudhary
ZINDAGI

Har khamoshi ko kamzori mat samajhna, Kuch alfaaz dil mein bhi raha karte hain. Jo har baat keh nahi paate, Wahi log aksar gehra mehsoos karte hain.

— Vinayak Chaudhary
